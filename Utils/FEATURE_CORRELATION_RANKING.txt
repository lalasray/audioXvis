KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
- Top 5 features (mel spec mean, MFCC_0, RMS, ZCR, spectral contrast) all
  show moderate correlation (~0.40-0.44). These energy/loudness/timbral
  features track angle changes best.

- angle_a and angle_c are more correlated with audio than angle_b across
  the board.

- Pitch (F0) has surprisingly low correlation — larynx geometry seems more
  tied to loudness and spectral shape than pitch.

- Chroma and tempogram show essentially zero correlation with angles.

================================================================================
QUICK INTERPRETATION GUIDE
================================================================================

For Vocal Box Analysis, Look For:

PITCH/F0 CONTOUR:
  • Smooth contours = healthy voice
  • Irregular jumps = vocal stress or instability
  • Missing values = unvoiced segments (fricatives, silence)

MEL-SPECTROGRAM:
  • Bright colors = strong frequency content (formants)
  • Clear bands = clean vowels
  • Blurred/noisy = consonants or breathiness

HARMONIC/PERCUSSIVE SEPARATION:
  • Strong harmonic component = clear vowels
  • Strong percussive component = noisy consonants
  • Balanced = natural speech

MFCC:
  • Smooth changes = natural speech
  • Sharp changes = fricatives or stops
  • Consistent patterns = vowel stability

RMS ENERGY:
  • High peaks = loud/emphasized syllables
  • Low valleys = weak or unvoiced sounds
  • Smooth envelope = controlled vocal dynamics
